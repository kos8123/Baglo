Baglo Privacy Policy
隱私權政策

Last updated / 最後更新日期：2026 年 11 月 24 日

This Privacy Policy explains how Baglo (https://baglo.pages.dev), an educational paper-trading web app for Taiwan stocks, collects, uses, stores, shares and deletes your information. Baglo never handles real money and never connects to a brokerage account. 中文版本請見下方。

1. Information we collect

  • Account information: your email address and a unique account identifier, created when you register with email and password or sign in with Google.
  • Google user data (only if you choose Google Sign-In): through the openid, email and profile scopes, Google shares your Google account ID, email address, whether the email is verified, and basic profile information (name and profile picture URL). Baglo stores only the Google account ID, email address and email-verified flag. Baglo does not store your name or profile picture, and never receives your Google password.
  • Paper-trading data you create: virtual cash balance, simulated orders, positions, watchlists, AI strategy settings and backtest results.
  • Technical logs: IP address, browser type, request time and error messages, kept for security and troubleshooting.
Baglo never asks for credit card numbers, bank or brokerage accounts, national ID numbers or real trading credentials.

2. How we use Google user data

  • Your Google account ID and email address are used only to create your Baglo account, sign you in, keep your paper-trading data linked to you, and contact you about your account if needed.
  • Google user data is not used for advertising, is not sold, and is not used to develop, improve or train any AI or machine-learning model.
  • Google user data is not sent to the AI model (Gemini API). The AI feature only receives public market data (stock symbols, prices, technical indicators) and the simulated strategy settings you choose.
  • Baglo does not access Gmail, Google Drive, Contacts, Calendar, Photos or any other Google API beyond sign-in.
  • Baglo does not generate or process images of people and does not create any intimate or sexual imagery.

Limited Use disclosure: Baglo’s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

3. How we share data

We do not sell, rent or trade personal data. Data is shared only with service providers that run Baglo on our behalf: Amazon Web Services (Amazon Cognito for sign-in, AWS Lambda, Amazon DynamoDB and Amazon S3 for processing and storage), Cloudflare (website hosting), and Google (Google Sign-In; Gemini API for text analysis of public market data only). We may also disclose data if required by law.

4. Storage, security and retention

  • Data is stored on AWS in the United States (us-east-1) and transmitted only over HTTPS. Access is limited by AWS IAM permissions.
  • Account and paper-trading data are kept while your account is active.
  • Technical logs are automatically deleted after 7 days.
  • When you delete your account, your account record, Google-linked identity and paper-trading data are deleted within 30 days.

5. Your choices and data deletion

You can use the market charts without an account. You can revoke Baglo’s access to your Google account at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. To access, correct or delete your data, or to delete your account, email kos8123@gmail.com from the email address of your account. We will respond within 30 days.

6. Cookies and local storage

Baglo uses browser storage (LocalStorage / SessionStorage) and sign-in cookies only to keep you signed in and remember interface settings. Baglo does not use advertising or tracking cookies.

7. Children

Baglo is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their data.

8. Changes and contact

We will post updates on this page and change the date above. Questions: kos8123@gmail.com.

一、我們蒐集的資料

  • 帳號與登入資訊：電子郵件與帳號識別碼。若選擇 Google 登入，Google 會透過 openid、email、profile 權限提供 Google 帳號 ID、電子郵件、是否已驗證及基本個人資料；本平台僅保存 Google 帳號 ID、電子郵件與驗證狀態，不保存姓名、大頭貼或 Google 密碼。
  • 模擬交易與使用紀錄：虛擬資產配置、模擬委託、持倉、自選標的、AI 模擬設定及介面偏好。
  • 技術與安全紀錄：為維護服務及防止濫用，雲端服務可能記錄 IP 位址、瀏覽器或裝置資訊、存取時間、請求與錯誤日誌。
特別聲明：Baglo 僅提供模擬交易，不要求提供信用卡、銀行帳戶、證券帳戶、身分證字號或真實交易憑證。

二、資料使用目的

  • 提供登入、模擬交易、持倉、AI 分析與偏好設定功能。
  • 保存您的模擬紀錄，改善介面、策略驗證與系統穩定性。
  • 驗證身分、防止未授權存取、偵測濫用並維護資訊安全。
  • 處理技術支援、權利請求與必要的服務通知。

三、第三方服務與 API

本平台使用 Amazon Web Services 提供身分驗證、運算、儲存與內容傳遞，並可能使用 Google 登入、Gemini AI、富果及公開市場資料來源。市場資料服務主要接收股票代號等查詢條件；AI 分析服務可能接收公開行情、技術指標及使用者選定的模擬策略資料。除提供功能所必要或依法要求外，我們不出售、出租或交換您的個人資料作行銷用途。

使用 Google 登入時，Google 會依其隱私權政策處理驗證資料；Baglo 僅要求 openid、email、profile 基本權限，不存取 Gmail、Google Drive、聯絡人或其他 Google 服務。Google 使用者資料僅用於建立帳號與登入，不用於廣告、不出售，也不會傳送給 AI 模型或用於訓練任何 AI 模型。本平台對 Google API 所取得資訊的使用與傳輸，遵守 Google API Services User Data Policy，包括「有限使用（Limited Use）」規定。AI 功能僅產生文字分析，不產生或處理任何人物影像。

四、Cookie 與瀏覽器儲存

本平台可能使用 Cookie、LocalStorage 或 SessionStorage 完成安全登入流程、維持工作階段及記住介面設定。您可在瀏覽器清除這些資料，但登入與部分功能可能因此失效。

五、資料安全與保存

我們採用 HTTPS、受控的雲端權限及身分驗證機制保護資料。個人資料會於提供服務、維護安全及履行法定義務所需期間保存；技術日誌保存 7 天後自動刪除；帳號刪除請求完成後，帳號、Google 連結身分與模擬交易資料將於 30 天內刪除。您可隨時於 Google 帳戶權限頁面撤銷 Baglo 的存取權。

六、您的權利

依中華民國《個人資料保護法》，您可請求查詢或閱覽、製給複製本、補充或更正、停止蒐集、處理或利用，以及刪除個人資料。提出刪除帳號或其他資料請求時，我們可能先驗證您的身分。

七、兒童隱私

本平台並非以未滿 13 歲兒童為對象。如發現未經適當同意而蒐集兒童資料，請聯絡我們處理。

八、政策更新與聯絡方式

本政策更新時會公布於此頁並修改更新日期。如有隱私、帳號或資料刪除相關問題，請寄信至 kos8123@gmail.com。
